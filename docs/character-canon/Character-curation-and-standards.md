# Character curation — Working standard, 12 November 2026

Standardize around a small memorable cast with clear work. The internal editorial default is Moss for memory, Luma for design, Sable for research and Morel for capture. Keep Mini Frank / Nilo / Brio / Riff as the founder/editorial cast. Use the porcelain/navy three-arc Queen study as the working coordination direction; it does not replace the specialists or select a new public master.

These are design decisions for future briefs and reference selection. Existing identity authority, rights and publication states are unchanged. No audience study, brand-recognition test or commercial performance result is claimed.

## Standards

- One material grammar: tactile porcelain, satin brass/copper, optically convincing glass, timber, paper and restrained living ecology.
- Keep identity anchors across editions: Moss shell/face; Luma orchid crown; Sable ears/tail; Morel cap/body; Nilo camera eyes; Brio goggles/antenna; Riff lobster form.
- Use daylight for making, overcast garden light for memory/ecology, and controlled evening light for research. Avoid making every scene amber, ornate and crowded.
- Keep Instrument Sans for interface text, Instrument Serif for selective editorial accents, IBM Plex Mono for technical labels, and current semantic UI tokens.
- Write exact text, numbers, source labels, diagrams and UI in native Figma/HTML. Artwork supplies character and atmosphere.
- Use a static, quiet equivalent; a character cannot block a task. Small operator surfaces show compact status and real controls before decorative scenes.
- Avoid square-cropping a wide ensemble. Preserve 3:2 scene composition and select an explicitly reviewed portrait for 32/64/160 px uses.
- A scene is concept art; rendered workrooms are not editable 3D models. Character appearances do not establish tool access or successful execution.

## Use by surface

**Operator / product:** Four specialists in their relevant task; Queen only for coordination; quiet mode is available. Avoid: Full-screen cinematic interruption, fake UI, decorative authority or another new face per control.

**Horizon / institution:** One shared mission and the campus to show a possible future with a concrete entry point. Avoid: Using a fantasy city or environmental scene as proof of actual impact.

**Academy / learning:** Core cast and specialist demonstrations with a visible artifact and clear next action. Avoid: Confusing role names, model providers, runtime agents and character identity.

**Cultural / community:** Riff, ensemble scenes, gardens and making narratives with more emotional range. Avoid: Borrowed vendor identity, cross-brand canon leakage and unselected founder renditions.

**FrankX / founder editorial:** Mini Frank with Nilo, Brio and Riff from the established source sheets. Avoid: Substituting any attractive new face or changing the cast through a generated group scene.

Keep the supplied source separately as a cultural composition reference; its energy is useful, while vendor avatars and likeness approval remain separate.
- All ten generated explainer posters: composition references only. Rebuild text natively and re-check the character anatomy. Sable and Pico need particular limb review.
- Vendor source artwork: attributed comparison only where allowed, never owned Starlight identity.
- Brand-direction boards and Anime Legends / Arcanea sources: owning-brand review, not Starlight mascot selection.
- SL-ECO-28: mood scene, not an Enoki proportion reference. SL-ECO-05/29: editorial scene, not full-body extraction.
- Broad casts, crown-like decoration and ornate workrooms inside dense operator controls: use a small relevant guide or quiet state instead.

## Leverage first

- Make memory/capture kits legible with Moss/Morel covers, useful editable files and one short illustrated workflow.
